As of writing this (May 1st 2026) I am no longer going to work on this game, it is effectively cancelled. The code's spaghetti and the GameMaker project is a total mess, making it unbearable to work on this. The engine and IDE have also gotten way to heavy for my low-end computer. I'll be moving to Godot for future projects but don't expect anything more from Frebby in Space. I'm trully sorry but I just can't do this anymore. Best Regards, Robert.
